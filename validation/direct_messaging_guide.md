# Direct Messaging Guide - "Open to Work" Strategy

**Question:** Should I message people with "Open to Work" badges?

**Answer:** YES - they're your exact target audience. BUT do it carefully to avoid spam flags.

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: LinkedIn Limits & Risks

### LinkedIn Messaging Limits

**Free LinkedIn:**
- **Connection requests:** 100-200 per week (soft limit)
- **Messages to 2nd/3rd degree:** Very limited
- **InMail:** 0 (requires Premium)

**LinkedIn Premium/Sales Nav:**
- **Connection requests:** ~200 per week
- **InMail credits:** 5-50/month (depending on tier)
- **Open Profile messages:** Unlimited (if they have Open Profile)

### Spam Flags to Avoid

**LinkedIn will restrict your account if:**
- ❌ Too many connection requests rejected (>40% reject rate)
- ❌ People mark your messages as spam
- ❌ You send identical messages to many people
- ❌ High volume in short time (>50 messages/day)
- ❌ Low acceptance rate on connection requests (<50%)

**Safe approach:**
- ✅ Personalize every message
- ✅ Only message 2nd-degree connections (mutual connections)
- ✅ Limit to 20-30 messages per day
- ✅ Vary message timing (not all at once)
- ✅ High-quality targets only

---

## 🎯 Should You Do This?

### ✅ DO THIS IF:
- You have LinkedIn Premium or Sales Navigator
- You have time to personalize messages (30-60 min)
- You want higher-quality responses
- You need to hit 50-100 more responses
- You're comfortable with direct outreach

### ❌ SKIP THIS IF:
- You're already hitting response targets from Reddit/Groups
- You don't have Premium (free account limits are too restrictive)
- You don't want to risk spam flags
- You're not comfortable with cold outreach

### My Recommendation:
**Use this as a supplement, not primary distribution method.**
- Reddit/Groups: 300-500 responses (your main source)
- Direct messages: 30-60 bonus responses (targeted, high-quality)

## 👥 Who to Target

### Ideal Profile:
```
✅ "Open to Work" badge visible
✅ 2nd-degree connection (you have mutual connections)
✅ Relevant role: PM, Designer, Engineer, Sales, Marketing
✅ Active on LinkedIn (posted in last 30 days)
✅ 500+ connections (active networkers)
✅ Located in US/your target market
✅ Senior IC or Manager level (less junior, more senior)
```

### How to Find Them:

**Method 1: LinkedIn Search**
1. Go to LinkedIn search
2. Click "People"
3. Filters:
   - **2nd degree** (better response rate)
   - **Open to Work** badge
   - **Location:** United States (or your target)
   - **Current company:** (Leave blank to find job seekers)
   - **Past company:** Google, Meta, Amazon, etc. (high-quality targets)
   - **Title:** Product Manager, Software Engineer, etc.

**Method 2: Your Network's Networks**
1. Go to "My Network" → "Connections"
2. Click on a connection who has many connections
3. View their connections
4. Look for "Open to Work" badges
5. Send connection request with note

**Method 3: Sales Navigator (If you have it)**
1. Advanced search
2. Filter: "Open to opportunities"
3. Filter: "Posted on LinkedIn in last 30 days"
4. Filter: "2nd degree connections"
5. Save search, message 10-20/day

## 📝 Message Templates

### Template 1: Connection Request (Recommended)

**Use this if:** You're NOT yet connected

**Character limit:** 300 characters

```
Hi [First Name],

Saw you're exploring new opportunities. I'm researching how job seekers approach LinkedIn content creation.

Would love your input on a 2-min survey (+ chance to win $50 Amazon card).

Can I share the link if you're interested?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Character count:** ~220 (leaves room for personalization)

**Why this works:**
- Acknowledges their job search
- Offers value (research + gift card)
- Asks permission (not pushy)
- Short enough to read quickly

## 📋 Workflow

### Step-by-Step Process:

**1. Create Target List (30 min)**
- Search for "Open to Work" + filters
- Export 50-100 profiles to a spreadsheet
- Prioritize: 2nd-degree > 3rd-degree
- Note: Mutual connections, recent activity

**2. Personalize Messages (15-20 min)**
- Open 10 profiles
- Find one personalization point per person:
  - Recent post they made
  - Company they worked at
  - Skill/background you share
  - Mutual connection
- Write custom intro line for each

**3. Send Messages (10-15 min)**
- Copy template
- Add personalization
- Add their name
- Send
- Track in spreadsheet

**4. Track Responses (5 min daily)**
- Note who responded
- Thank them
- Answer questions

## 🚨 Red Flags to Stop

### STOP messaging if:

**Warning Sign 1: Low acceptance rate**
- <50% of connection requests accepted
- **Action:** Stop sending requests, focus on existing connections

**Warning Sign 2: People marking as spam**
- LinkedIn sends you a warning
- **Action:** Immediately stop, wait 1 week, be more personal

**Warning Sign 3: Account restriction**
- LinkedIn limits your messaging
- **Action:** Stop immediately, wait for restriction to lift

**Warning Sign 4: Low response rate**
- <20% of messages get responses
- **Action:** Your message isn't resonating, revise template

**Warning Sign 5: Negative replies**
- People saying "This is spam"
- **Action:** Stop, your approach is too aggressive

## ✅ Best Practices

### Do This:

1. ✅ **Personalize every single message**
   - Find one unique thing about them
   - Reference it in first line

2. ✅ **Use their first name**
   - Not "Hi there" or "Hello"
   - "Hi Sarah," or "Hey Mike,"

3. ✅ **Acknowledge their situation**
   - "I know job searching is tough"
   - "Hope your search is going well"

4. ✅ **Make it about them, not you**
   - "Would love your perspective"
   - Not "I need survey responses"

5. ✅ **Keep it short**
   - 100-150 words max
   - Easy to skim

6. ✅ **Offer value**
   - Gift card raffle
   - Early access to tool
   - Survey results

7. ✅ **Give them an out**
   - "No pressure if you're busy!"
   - Shows empathy

---

### Don't Do This:

1. ❌ **Copy/paste identical messages**
   - LinkedIn can detect this
   - Gets flagged as spam

2. ❌ **Send to 1st-degree connections without context**
   - Feels impersonal
   - They'll wonder how you got their info

3. ❌ **Be pushy**
   - "Can you do this now?"
   - "I really need this"

4. ❌ **Only ask for favors**
   - Offer to help them too
   - Share your network, insights

5. ❌ **Message too many people at once**
   - 20-30/day max
   - Spread over week
