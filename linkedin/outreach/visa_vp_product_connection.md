# Connection Message: VP of Product Management, Visa

## Target
- **Title**: VP of Product Management
- **Company**: Visa
- **Seniority**: VP/Executive
- **Tone**: Brief, high-impact (1-2 sentences per guidelines)

---

## Variation 1: Project-Specific Admiration (Recommended)
```
Hi [NAME],

Visa's work on generative AI across payment experiences is compelling — I've led similar AI transformation efforts at a Series B marketplace and saw $3.6M+ in impact. Would love to connect.

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- References Visa's known AI/ML investment in payments
- Establishes peer credibility with a concrete, verified metric
- Short and respectful of executive time

**When to Use**:
- After Visa announces an AI-related product or initiative
- When they've shared or engaged with AI/payments content

---

## Variation 4: Mutual Connection Bridge
```
Hi [NAME],

[MUTUAL_CONNECTION] mentioned the exciting product work happening at Visa. I've been leading AI and platform product efforts in the marketplace space and would love to connect.

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- Social proof reduces stranger-danger at the VP level
- Brief and credibility-forward
- Highest acceptance rate formula for executives

**When to Use**:
- Only when you have a genuine mutual connection
- After confirming the mutual is comfortable being referenced

```

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- Name-drops a specific, emerging platform (Panso) showing you track the ecosystem closely

- Leverages your unique hospitality + marketplace + payments crossover experience

**When to Use**:

- After Visa announces partnerships or updates to in-person payment infrastructure
- When the VP engages with hospitality tech or POS-related content

- Integrates with Lightspeed POS to sync transaction and guest data
- Covers event sales, ticketing, marketing (email/SMS), and guest intelligence
- Represents the trend of hospitality platforms needing unified payment + data layers — exactly what Visa's acceptance solutions enable
