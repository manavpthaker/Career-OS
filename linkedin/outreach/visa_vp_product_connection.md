# Connection Message: VP of Product Management, Visa

## Target
- **Title**: VP of Product Management
- **Company**: Visa
- **Seniority**: VP/Executive
- **Tone**: Brief, high-impact (1-2 sentences per guidelines)

---

## Variation 1: Project-Specific Admiration (Recommended)
```
Hi [NAME],

Visa's work on generative AI across payment experiences is compelling — I've led similar AI transformation efforts at a Series B marketplace and saw $3.6M+ in impact. Would love to connect.

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- References Visa's known AI/ML investment in payments
- Establishes peer credibility with a concrete, verified metric
- Short and respectful of executive time

**When to Use**:
- After Visa announces an AI-related product or initiative
- When they've shared or engaged with AI/payments content

---

## Variation 2: Shared Challenge (Platform Focus)
```
Hi [NAME],

Fascinating how Visa is evolving its developer platform and APIs. I've spent the last few years scaling marketplace platforms and building AI-powered tooling — would love to connect and follow your team's work.

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- References Visa Developer Platform, a real strategic priority
- Aligns API/platform experience without overselling
- "Follow your team's work" is low-pressure and genuine

**When to Use**:
- If the VP oversees developer platform or API products
- After engaging with Visa developer-related content

---

## Variation 3: Industry Insight (Payments + AI)
```
Hi [NAME],

The intersection of AI and payments infrastructure is where I see the biggest product opportunities right now. Leading AI initiatives at a marketplace gave me a front-row seat — would value connecting with someone shaping that space at Visa's scale.

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- Positions you as a thinker in their problem space
- Flattery with substance (acknowledges Visa's scale and influence)
- Signals strategic perspective appropriate for VP-level conversation

**When to Use**:
- For broader networking when you don't have a specific project reference
- When the VP posts about payments industry trends

---

## Variation 4: Mutual Connection Bridge
```
Hi [NAME],

[MUTUAL_CONNECTION] mentioned the exciting product work happening at Visa. I've been leading AI and platform product efforts in the marketplace space and would love to connect.

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- Social proof reduces stranger-danger at the VP level
- Brief and credibility-forward
- Highest acceptance rate formula for executives

**When to Use**:
- Only when you have a genuine mutual connection
- After confirming the mutual is comfortable being referenced

---

## Follow-Up Plan (If Accepted)

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Brief thank you — no ask |
| 3-5 | Like or comment on one of their recent posts |
| 7-10 | Share a relevant article or insight on AI in payments |
| 14+ | If engagement is mutual, mention exploring opportunities |

## Pre-Outreach Warm-Up (Recommended)
- Engage with 2-3 Visa product team posts before sending the request
- Comment thoughtfully on any content the VP shares
- Like recent company announcements related to product/AI

---

## Variation 5: Hospitality + Payments Background
```
Hi [NAME],

Having built POS and payment products at Panso in hospitality, I've seen firsthand how critical Visa's infrastructure is to the merchant experience. Would love to connect.

Manav
```

**Why This Works**:
- Naturally establishes you as someone who's worked in the payments/POS space
- Creates a direct bridge to Visa's world without pitching anything
- Brief and VP-appropriate

**When to Use**:
- If the VP works on acceptance solutions or merchant-facing products
- When you want to lead with domain credibility over AI/marketplace experience

---

## Notes
- **Do NOT** mention job seeking in the connection request
- **Do NOT** ask for referrals or favors
- **Character limit**: LinkedIn connection messages have a 300-character limit — all variations above fit within this
- **Profile check**: Ensure your LinkedIn headline and summary reflect AI/platform PM expertise before sending
